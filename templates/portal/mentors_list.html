How the score works

Every rate shows “–” when it has no denominator. A missing rate is left out of the score and the remaining weights are rescaled, so lack of data never counts as 0% or 100%. A mentor is only ranked once enough tests were due in the period.

  • Tests conducted ({{ weights.conduct }}%) = completed ÷ due tests (date has passed). Cancelled and missed tests count against the rate; reasons are visible on each test.
  • Marks entered ({{ weights.marks }}%) = completed tests with marks for every active student ÷ completed tests.
  • Schedule compliance ({{ weights.compliance }}%) = batches within their test interval ÷ the mentor's active batches.
  • Tasks done ({{ weights.task_rate }}%) = completed ÷ tasks assigned in the period.
  • Tasks on time ({{ weights.on_time }}%) = tasks finished by their deadline ÷ tasks with a deadline that are finished or overdue.
